StaffGlimpse Performance Management helps your organization align employee work with business outcomes through structured goal setting, continuous feedback, and periodic review cycles. Whether you run quarterly OKRs or annual appraisals, the module gives HR teams, managers, and employees a single place to track progress and document growth.

## What you can do

- **Set and align goals**: Create OKRs or KPIs at the organization, team, and individual level, then link individual goals to higher-level objectives so everyone sees how their work contributes to company priorities.
- **Run continuous feedback**: Managers and peers can share feedback at any time, not just during review season, so employees get timely coaching and recognition.
- **Launch review cycles**: Configure self-reviews, manager reviews, and peer reviews into a single cycle with due dates, templates, and rating scales.
- **Track progress**: View goal completion rates, review participation, and calibration summaries from a central dashboard.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Review cycle | A time-bound performance period (for example, Q1 or FY 2024-25) that contains goals and reviews. |
| Goal | A measurable objective assigned to an employee, with a weight and a target. |
| Template | A reusable review form that defines the questions, rating scale, and reviewer types for a cycle. |
| Calibration | A step where managers and HR review proposed ratings together to ensure fairness before sharing results. |
