Launch a structured performance review cycle in StaffGlimpse to collect self-assessments, manager evaluations, and peer feedback in one workflow. After reviews are submitted, HR and leadership can calibrate ratings to ensure fairness before sharing final scores with employees.

## Prerequisites

- You must have the **Admin** or **HR** role.
- An active review cycle timeframe must exist. If you have not created one yet, [set up a review cycle first](/performance/goals).

## Configure a review template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open templates">
    Go to **Performance** > **Settings** > **Review Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a template">
    Click **New Template**. Enter a name (for example, "Annual Review 2025").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add reviewer types">
    Under **Reviewers**, enable the types you want:

    - **Self review**: the employee evaluates their own performance.
    - **Manager review**: the direct manager evaluates the employee.
    - **Peer review**: colleagues provide feedback, usually anonymously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the form">
    Add sections and questions. For each question, choose a response type: **Rating scale** (1 to 5), **Free text**, or **Goal-based** (auto-populated from the employee's goals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the rating scale">
    Define the overall rating scale (for example, 1 to 5) and label each level (for example, 1 = Needs Improvement, 5 = Exceptional). Click **Save Template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Launch the review cycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the cycle">
    Go to **Performance** > **Review Cycles**. Select the cycle you created earlier, then click **Launch**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the template">
    Choose the review template you configured. Select the employee population: all employees, a department, or a custom list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set deadlines">
    Enter due dates for each phase: self-review, manager review, peer review, and calibration. Click **Publish** to send notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete reviews

<Steps>
  <Step title="Employees submit self-reviews">
    Each employee receives an email and an in-app task. They open **My Reviews**, answer the questions, and click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Managers complete their reviews">
    Managers open **Performance** > **Team Reviews**. They see a list of direct reports. For each employee, they review the self-assessment, answer the manager questions, and assign an overall rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Peers provide feedback">
    If peer review is enabled, nominated peers receive a link to a short form. Peer feedback is collected and shown to the manager in aggregate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enable **Anonymous peer feedback** in the template settings so peers can share honest observations without attribution. Managers still see the full text, but the peer names are hidden.
</Tip>

## Calibrate and share ratings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run calibration">
    HR opens **Performance** > **Calibration**. Select the cycle and view a grid of all employees with their proposed ratings. Adjust ratings after discussion with managers, then lock the final scores.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share results">
    Click **Share Results** to publish ratings to employees. Each employee receives a notification that their review is ready and can view their scorecard in **My Reviews**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Employees can view their final rating, manager comments, and peer feedback summary. HR can export the full cycle data for compensation planning or succession discussions.
