Use StaffGlimpse Goals to define what success looks like for each employee during a review cycle. You can create organization-level objectives, break them into team and individual goals, assign weights, and track progress as employees update their key results.

## Prerequisites

- You must have the **Admin** or **HR** role, or a custom role with **Performance** module access.
- A review cycle timeframe must be active before you can assign goals.

## Create a review cycle timeframe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Performance settings">
    From the main navigation, go to **Performance** > **Settings** > **Review Cycles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new cycle">
    Click **New Cycle**. Enter a name (for example, "Q1 2025"), choose a start date and end date, then select the goal template you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Save**. Toggle the cycle status to **Active** so managers and employees can begin adding goals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add goals for an employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the employee's goal sheet">
    Go to **Performance** > **Goals**. Select an employee from the dropdown, or ask a manager to open their team list and click **Goals** next to the employee name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a goal">
    Click **Add Goal**. Enter a title, description, and the target value. Choose a measurement type: **Percentage**, **Number**, **Currency**, or **Completed / Not completed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set weight and alignment">
    Enter a **Weight** (for example, 25) so the goal contributes proportionally to the overall rating. Under **Aligned to**, select an organization or team objective from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign and notify">
    Choose the **Assignee** (the employee who owns the goal). Optionally add a **Reviewer** who will validate completion. Click **Save and Notify** to send an email and in-app alert.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track progress

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update key results">
    The employee opens **My Goals** from their dashboard, selects a goal, and clicks **Update Progress**. They enter the current value and add a note about what was accomplished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review progress as a manager">
    Managers open **Performance** > **Team Goals** to see a consolidated view. Click any employee to view their goal completion percentage and recent updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run reports">
    HR and admins can go to **Performance** > **Reports** > **Goal Summary** to export a CSV of all goals by department, cycle, and completion rate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Employees see their goals on the home dashboard with progress bars. Managers receive weekly digest emails for their team. HR can export goal attainment reports before starting a review cycle.
