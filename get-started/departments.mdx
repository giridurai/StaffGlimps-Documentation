Departments in StaffGlimpse group employees by function (for example, Engineering, Sales, Finance) so you can route approvals, scope permissions, and filter reports. This page walks you through creating departments, nesting sub-departments, and assigning department heads.

## Prerequisites

- Admin or HR role with access to **Organization settings**.
- Your [organization details](/get-started/organization-settings) are saved.
- A list of departments and (optionally) their heads.

## Create a department

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Departments">
    Go to **Settings > Organization > Departments**. You'll see the current department list and the org tree view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new department">
    Click **Add Department**. Enter the **Department name** (for example, `Engineering`), an optional **Code** (`ENG`), and a short **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a department head">
    Pick an existing employee from the **Head of Department** dropdown. The head receives approval requests routed to the department by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the parent department (optional)">
    Leave **Parent** blank for a top-level department, or select a parent to create a sub-department (for example, `Engineering > Platform`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to work locations">
    Choose one or more **Locations** where this department operates. Location links drive location-scoped permissions and reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The department is now available when onboarding employees and when creating roles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The new department appears in the department list and org tree. You can now assign it during [employee onboarding](/employees/onboarding) and use it to scope [roles and permissions](/admin/roles-permissions).

## Nest sub-departments

Sub-departments inherit the parent's location links and appear indented in the org tree.

<Tip>
  Keep the hierarchy shallow (2 to 3 levels). Deep nesting makes approval routing and reports harder to read.
</Tip>

To move a department under a different parent, open the department and change the **Parent** field, or drag it in the org tree view.

## Bulk import departments

For large orgs, import departments in bulk instead of adding them one by one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the template">
    On the Departments page, click **Import > Download template**. The CSV includes `name`, `code`, `parent_code`, `head_email`, and `locations`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the CSV">
    Add one row per department. Use `parent_code` to nest sub-departments and `head_email` to assign heads by their StaffGlimpse email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Click **Import > Upload CSV**. Review the preview, fix any validation errors, and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit or archive a department

<Warning>
  You cannot delete a department that has active employees. Reassign employees first, then archive the department to keep historical records intact.
</Warning>

- **Edit**: Open the department, change fields, and save.
- **Archive**: Open the department and click **Archive**. Archived departments stay in past payroll runs and reports but no longer appear in dropdowns.
