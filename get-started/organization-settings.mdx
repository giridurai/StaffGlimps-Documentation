Before you invite employees or process payroll, you must configure your organization profile in StaffGlimpse. This page covers how to enter your company details, legal identifiers, work locations, and regional preferences. Accurate organization data ensures correct statutory reporting and payslip formatting.

## Prerequisites

- An active StaffGlimpse admin account
- Your company PAN, TAN, and registered address
- A list of work locations (head office and branches)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization settings">
    Sign in to StaffGlimpse and click **Settings** > **Organization** from the left sidebar. This page shows your current organization profile or a blank form if you are setting up for the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter company details">
    Fill in the **Company Name** and **Legal Entity Name** exactly as they appear on your registration documents. Enter your **PAN** and **TAN** in the respective fields. These identifiers are used on payslips and statutory filings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add registered and billing address">
    Provide your full registered address under **Address Details**. If your billing address differs, check **Billing address is different** and enter the secondary address. This address appears on invoices and compliance reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up work locations">
    Click **Add Location** under the Work Locations section. Enter the location name, complete address, and state. You can add multiple locations if your company operates across branches. Each employee can be assigned to a specific location for attendance and payroll grouping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose financial year and currency">
    Select your **Financial Year Start Month** from the dropdown (commonly April for Indian companies). Set the **Default Currency** to INR. The financial year drives payroll periods, leave accrual cycles, and tax computation timelines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time zone">
    Pick your organization's primary **Time Zone** from the list. Attendance logs, leave requests, and payroll cutoffs are evaluated against this time zone. If you have teams in other zones, you can configure location-level overrides later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and review">
    Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page. Review the summary card on the right to confirm all details are correct. You can edit these settings later, but changes to the financial year may affect historical payroll reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set the correct financial year before you run your first payroll cycle. Changing the financial year after payroll is processed can shift tax computation periods and affect TDS and PT calculations. If you are unsure, confirm the start month with your finance team before saving.
</Tip>

## Result

Your organization profile is complete. StaffGlimpse now recognizes your company identity, locations, and regional settings. You can proceed to add employees and configure payroll structures.
