Your StaffGlimpse admin account is the gateway to managing your organization's HR and payroll data. This guide shows you how to create the account, verify your identity, and secure it with two-factor authentication. Only users with the admin role can create organizations and manage billing.

## Prerequisites

- A valid work email address you can access immediately
- An authenticator app installed on your mobile device (for 2FA)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for StaffGlimpse">
    Visit the [StaffGlimpse registration page](https://staffglimpse.com) and enter your work email, full name, and a strong password. Click **Create Account**. If your company already uses StaffGlimpse, ask your admin to send you an invite link instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from StaffGlimpse. Click the **Verify Email** button inside the message. If you do not see the email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. The link expires after 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your admin password">
    After verification, you are prompted to set a secure password. Choose at least 12 characters with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. This password protects your admin dashboard and API access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication">
    Open **Account Settings** > **Security** and toggle **Two-Factor Authentication**. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, enter the six-digit code to confirm, and save your recovery codes in a secure location. StaffGlimpse requires 2FA for all admin accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your admin role">
    Navigate to **Administration** > **Roles & Permissions** to confirm you are assigned the **Organization Admin** role. This role lets you create the organization, invite team members, and manage billing. If the role is missing, contact your account manager or the person who invited you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admin accounts have full access to employee data, payroll records, and billing settings. Do not share your login credentials or recovery codes with anyone.
</Note>

## Result

Your StaffGlimpse admin account is active and secured with two-factor authentication. You can now proceed to [create your organization](/get-started/organization-settings) and invite other team members.
