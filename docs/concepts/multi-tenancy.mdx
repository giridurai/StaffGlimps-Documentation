StaffGlimpse is a multi-tenant platform, meaning every company that signs up gets its own fully isolated environment — its own tenant. All data within your account, including users, departments, attendance records, leave requests, and documents, is automatically scoped to your tenant based on the identity embedded in your Cognito ID token. You never juggle tenant identifiers in your request payloads; authentication handles the association for you.

## What is a Tenant?

A tenant is your company account on StaffGlimpse. It comes into existence the moment you complete registration via `POST /company/register`. From that point on, every API call you make is automatically bound to your tenant — the Cognito ID token you include in the `Authorization` header carries your company identity, and the platform resolves your tenant context server-side before processing the request.

<Note>
  You never need to pass a tenant ID explicitly in your request body or query
  parameters. Your Bearer token is the tenant identifier.
</Note>

## Tenant Isolation

Each tenant's data is completely isolated from every other tenant on the platform. The following resources belong exclusively to your company and are never visible to — or reachable by — any other tenant:

- **Users & Profiles** — employee accounts and their personal data
- **Departments & Roles** — your organisational structure and custom roles
- **Leave Policies & Requests** — your company-specific leave rules and employee submissions
- **Attendance Records** — punch-in/out logs and computed timesheets
- **Documents** — uploaded files and their metadata

No cross-tenant data access is possible at the API layer. Even if two tenants share the same department name or role title, their underlying records are stored and served independently.

## Account URL

When your company is registered, StaffGlimpse assigns it a unique `AccountURL` slug — a short, URL-friendly identifier for your organisation (for example, `acme`). This slug is returned in the `GET /company` response and is visible in your dashboard at [https://staffglimpse.com](https://staffglimpse.com). You can use it to identify your tenant when working with support or navigating the dashboard.

```bash
GET https://r00buwtnh1.execute-api.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/live/company
Authorization: Bearer <AWS_Cognito_ID_Token>
x-api-key: <static_shared_key>
```

## Company Profile Fields

The `GET /company` endpoint returns your full tenant profile. The table below describes the key fields you will find in the `result` object:

| Field            | Type     | Description                                                                 |
| ---------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `CompanyName`    | `string` | The registered legal name of your company.                                  |
| `Email`          | `string` | The primary contact email address for your account.                         |
| `AccountURL`     | `string` | Your unique tenant slug, used to identify your company in the dashboard.    |
| `Plan`           | `string` | The subscription plan your company is currently on.                         |
| `Status`         | `string` | Account status — typically `Active`, `Inactive`, or `Suspended`.            |
| `BillCycle`      | `string` | Your billing frequency, e.g., `Monthly` or `Annually`.                      |
| `ExpiringDate`   | `string` | ISO 8601 datetime on which your current plan period expires.                |
| `DomainStatus`   | `string` | Indicates whether your custom domain (if configured) has been verified.     |
| `CreatedDate`    | `string` | ISO 8601 datetime when your company account was first registered.           |
| `Image`          | `string` | URL of your company logo image, if one has been uploaded.                   |
| `PaymentMethod`  | `string` | The payment method on file for your subscription, e.g., `Credit Card`.     |
| `InvoiceID`      | `string` | The identifier of the most recent invoice for your account.                 |
| `Amount`         | `string` | The billing amount associated with your current plan period.                |

## Subscription Plans

Your current subscription plan and billing cycle are reflected in the `Plan` and `BillCycle` fields of your company profile. StaffGlimpse offers multiple plans with different feature sets and user limits. To upgrade, downgrade, or make any billing changes, contact StaffGlimpse support through the in-dashboard support channel at [https://staffglimpse.com](https://staffglimpse.com).

<Tip>
  Use `GET /company` at startup to verify your tenant configuration and check
  your account status before making other API calls. If `Status` is not
  `Active`, downstream calls may fail or return limited data.
</Tip>
