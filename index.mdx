StaffGlimpse is a multi-tenant HR and Payroll SaaS platform built for HR managers, admins, and payroll teams at small-to-mid businesses. It covers the full employee lifecycle, from onboarding and attendance tracking to payroll processing, statutory compliance, and performance management. Whether you are setting up your first HR policy or scaling across multiple locations, StaffGlimpse gives you a single place to manage people, pay, and compliance.

## What you can do with StaffGlimpse

- **Employee management**: Onboard new hires, maintain digital profiles, manage documents, and handle offboarding workflows.
- **Attendance and leave**: Track attendance with multiple policies, manage leave balances, and configure holiday calendars.
- **Payroll processing**: Define salary structures, run monthly payroll, generate payslips, and handle statutory deductions like PF, ESI, TDS, and PT.
- **Performance management**: Set employee goals, schedule review cycles, and capture feedback.
- **Administration**: Control roles and permissions, manage billing, and enforce security policies.
- **API access**: Integrate employee, attendance, and payroll data into your own systems using the StaffGlimpse REST API.
