Employee profiles in StaffGlimpse hold the complete record for each person in your organization. HR admins and managers can update employment and salary details, while employees can maintain their own contact information, address, and documents through the self-service portal.

## What you can update

- **Personal info**: Name, date of birth, gender, marital status, contact number, and residential address.
- **Employment details**: Department, designation, location, reporting manager, joining date, and employee ID.
- **Salary details**: CTC, basic pay, allowances, deductions, and bank account for salary transfer.
- **Documents**: Government ID, PAN, education certificates, and previous employment records.
- **Family and nominees**: Spouse, children, and emergency contacts; PF and insurance nominees for statutory compliance.

## Profile views

StaffGlimpse shows different fields and edit permissions depending on who is viewing the profile.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Employee view">
    Employees can edit their own **contact number**, **address**, **bank account**, and upload **documents**. They can view their salary breakdown and download payslips, but they cannot change employment details, CTC, or reporting manager.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="HR view">
    HR admins and HR managers have full edit access. They can update **employment details**, **salary structure**, **statutory numbers** (UAN, ESI, PAN), **family and nominee information**, and change the employee's **status** (for example, from Active to Offboarding).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to update a profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the employee profile">
    Go to **Employees > Directory**, search for the employee, and click their name to open the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the relevant section">
    Click the **Edit** button on the section you want to update: **Personal Info**, **Employment Details**, **Salary**, **Documents**, or **Family and Nominees**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Make your updates and click **Save**. Changes to salary or employment details are typically logged in an audit trail for compliance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The profile is updated immediately. Employees see their own changes in the portal right away. HR-driven updates to salary or reporting structure are reflected in the next payroll run and the org chart.
