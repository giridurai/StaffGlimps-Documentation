The Employees module in StaffGlimpse is your central hub for managing every person in your organization. From the first day of onboarding through the final offboarding steps, you maintain a single source of truth for employee data, documents, reporting lines, and custom attributes.

## What you can do

- **Employee directory**: Browse, search, and filter all employees by department, location, designation, or status.
- **Lifecycle stages**: Track employees through onboarding, active employment, and offboarding.
- **Custom fields**: Capture additional data unique to your organization, such as project codes, cost centers, or badge numbers.
- **Org chart**: Visualize reporting hierarchies and explore team structures with an interactive organization chart.

## Key concepts

| Concept | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Employee status | Onboarding, Active, or Offboarding. Status controls access levels and payroll eligibility. |
| Custom fields | Configurable fields added by HR admins to store organization-specific data. |
| Org chart | A visual hierarchy built automatically from the Manager field on each employee profile. |
| Access roles | HR, Manager, and Employee views control what data each user can see and edit. |
