When an employee resigns, use the offboarding workflow in StaffGlimpse to manage the exit process systematically. The workflow captures the resignation date, tracks clearance tasks, calculates the full and final settlement, and prepares the experience letter.

<Warning>
  Plan to revoke the employee's portal access and system credentials on their last working day. Delaying access revocation creates a security risk, especially for employees with access to payroll or sensitive employee data.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

- You must have the **HR Admin** or **HR Manager** role.
- The employee must be in **Active** status.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initiate the exit">
    Go to **Employees > Directory**, open the employee profile, and click **Initiate Offboarding**. Enter the **Resignation Date** and **Last Working Day**. The employee status changes to **Offboarding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the exit checklist">
    Open the **Exit Checklist** tab and assign clearance tasks to the relevant stakeholders:

    - **IT / Admin**: Collect company assets (laptop, ID card, access cards)
    - **Manager**: Confirm knowledge transfer completion and handover of responsibilities
    - **Finance**: Verify pending expense claims and advances
    - **HR**: Collect statutory documents and update nominee records if needed

    Mark each task complete as the respective owner confirms clearance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calculate full and final settlement">
    Navigate to the **Settlement** section. StaffGlimpse computes:

    - Unpaid salary for days worked in the final month
    - Leave encashment for accrued but unused leave (as per your company policy)
    - Deductions for notice period shortfall, pending advances, or recoveries
    - Statutory contributions (PF, ESI, TDS, PT) for the final month

    Review the breakdown and adjust any manual entries before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revoke access">
    On the last working day, disable the employee's portal login and notify the IT team to revoke email, system, and building access. This step is critical for data security.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the experience letter">
    After all checklist items are cleared and the settlement is approved, click **Generate Experience Letter**. StaffGlimpse creates a letter with the employee's tenure, designation, and last working day. Download the PDF and share it with the employee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The employee status changes to **Inactive** after the last working day. The full and final settlement is ready for payout in the next payroll cycle. The experience letter and settlement statement are stored in the employee's document archive for future reference.
