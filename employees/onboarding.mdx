Use the onboarding workflow in StaffGlimpse to bring a new hire into your organization with the right documents, access, and reporting structure in place before their first day.

<Note>
  StaffGlimpse supports both **pre-onboarding** (before the joining date) and **post-joining** flows. Pre-onboarding lets you collect documents and send the portal invite early. Post-joining is used when the employee has already started and you need to record them in the system.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- You must have the **HR Admin** or **HR Manager** role.
- Department, designation, and location records should already be configured in **Organization Settings**.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the employee record">
    Go to **Employees > Directory** and click **Add Employee**. Enter the employee's full name, personal email, and mobile number. Select the **Joining Date** and choose whether to start in **Pre-onboarding** or **Active** status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign department, manager, and location">
    In the **Employment Details** section, select the employee's **Department**, **Designation**, **Location**, and **Reporting Manager**. These fields power the org chart and approval workflows for leave and expenses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect required documents">
    Open the **Documents** tab and request uploads for:

    - Government ID proof (Aadhaar, Passport, or Driving License)
    - PAN card
    - Bank account details for salary transfer
    - Previous employer relieving letter (if applicable)

    You can upload documents on behalf of the employee or send a request for them to upload through the employee portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set salary and statutory details">
    Navigate to the **Salary** section to enter the CTC, basic pay, and allowances. Add the employee's **UAN** (for PF), **ESI number** (if applicable), and **TDS declarations** so statutory compliance is ready before the first payroll run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the portal invite">
    Click **Send Invite** to deliver an email with a secure link to the employee portal. The invite lets the employee complete their profile, upload any pending documents, and view their payslips after the first payroll cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The new employee appears in the directory with an **Onboarding** or **Active** status. The org chart updates automatically to reflect the new reporting line. Pending document uploads are tracked on the employee profile until they are marked complete.
