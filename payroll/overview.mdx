StaffGlimpse Payroll helps you run accurate, compliant payroll for your organization every month. The module connects employee salary structures with attendance, leave, and statutory rules so you can compute earnings, deductions, and net pay in one place. Whether you are setting up a new grade structure or closing the monthly cycle, the payroll area keeps your books aligned with Indian compliance requirements.

## What you can do in Payroll

- **Build salary structures** with standard India components such as Basic, HRA, and Special Allowance, plus employer-side contributions like PF and ESI.
- **Run the monthly payroll cycle** by locking attendance, importing leave and LOP data, adding variables such as bonuses or arrears, and reviewing the computed sheet before approval.
- **Generate and publish payslips** for every employee with a detailed breakdown of earnings and deductions.
- **Stay compliant** with automatic computation of EPF, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS, and Labour Welfare Fund, plus ready-to-file reports and challans.
- **Handle full and final settlements** when employees exit, including prorated salary, encashments, and statutory adjustments.

## Key concepts

| Concept | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Salary structure | A template of fixed and variable pay components assigned to an employee or grade. |
| Payroll cycle | The monthly run that converts attendance and structure data into net pay. |
| Statutory deduction | Mandatory withholdings computed by StaffGlimpse based on current India slabs and limits. |
| Payslip | The published PDF statement of earnings and deductions for a given month. |
| F&F (Full and Final) | A special settlement run for exiting employees that includes prorated pay and recoveries. |
