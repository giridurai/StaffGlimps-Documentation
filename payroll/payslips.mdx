After payroll is approved, StaffGlimpse generates a detailed payslip for every employee. You control when payslips are published, and employees can view or download them from their self-service portal. You can also resend payslips by email if an employee requests a copy or if the original delivery failed.

## Prerequisites

- Payroll cycle approved for the month.
- Employee email addresses configured in their profiles.
- Portal access enabled for employees (self-service setting).

## Publish payslips

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payslip dashboard">
    Go to **Payroll > Payslips** and select the approved payroll month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview before publishing">
    Click **Preview** on any employee row to open the payslip PDF. Verify that the earnings, deductions, and net pay match the approved payroll sheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to all employees">
    Click **Publish All**. StaffGlimpse marks the payslips as available in the employee portal and sends an email notification to each employee with a secure download link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish selectively (optional)">
    If you need to withhold specific payslips, use the row-level **Publish** toggle for individual employees. You can publish them later without recomputing payroll.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Employee download

Once published, employees can log in to the StaffGlimpse self-service portal, navigate to **My Payslips**, and download any available payslip as a PDF. The portal shows a history of all published months and allows employees to filter by financial year.

<Tip>
  If an employee reports they cannot see a payslip, confirm that it has been published and that their portal access is active under **Employee Management > Portal Access**.
</Tip>

## Resend payslips by email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the employee">
    In **Payroll > Payslips**, select the month and search for the employee by name or ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend the email">
    Click the **Resend** icon next to the employee row. StaffGlimpse will immediately send a new email with the payslip download link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bulk resend (optional)">
    Select multiple employees using the checkboxes, then click **Resend Selected** to deliver payslips in one batch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Form 16 generation

StaffGlimpse generates Form 16 based on the full financial year payroll data. The generation window typically opens after the final payroll of the financial year is approved and TDS returns are reconciled.

<Note>
  Form 16 is usually available for download in the admin portal within a few weeks after the March payroll is closed. Employees receive an email notification once their Form 16 is published.
</Note>

## Result

Employees have access to their payslips in the portal and by email. HR retains an audit log of publish and resend actions for compliance.
