StaffGlimpse computes statutory deductions and contributions automatically during every payroll run. The system stays aligned with current India slabs and limits so your organization meets EPFO, ESIC, state PT, and income tax obligations without manual spreadsheets. This page explains what each statutory item covers, what StaffGlimpse calculates, and which reports or challans you can generate.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Provident Fund (EPF)">
    The Employees' Provident Fund is a retirement benefit where both employee and employer contribute a percentage of Basic wages each month. StaffGlimpse computes employee PF at 12% of Basic (subject to the statutory wage ceiling) and employer PF split into EPF and EPS portions. It also handles voluntary PF (VPF) if configured in the employee salary structure.

    **What StaffGlimpse generates:**

    - Monthly ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return) ready for EPFO upload.
    - Employee and employer PF summary by establishment.
    - Arrear PF adjustments when salary revisions are applied retrospectively.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Employees' State Insurance (ESI)">
    ESI provides medical and cash benefits to employees earning below a specified gross monthly threshold. StaffGlimpse checks gross wages against the current ESIC limit and applies employee contribution at 0.75% and employer contribution at 3.25% of gross when eligible.

    **What StaffGlimpse generates:**

    - Monthly ESIC contribution report with employee and employer totals.
    - Challan for online ESIC payment.
    - Eligibility flag on employee profiles when gross crosses the threshold.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Professional Tax (PT)">
    Professional Tax is a state-level tax deducted from employee salaries based on monthly gross slabs. StaffGlimpse uses the PT rules configured for your organization's registered state to apply the correct deduction amount and frequency (monthly or half-yearly).

    **What StaffGlimpse generates:**

    - Monthly PT deduction register by state and branch.
    - State-specific return and payment schedule reminders.
    - Exemption tracking for senior citizens or disabled employees where applicable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)">
    TDS on salary is computed under Section 192 of the Income Tax Act. StaffGlimpse projects annual taxable income from the salary structure, applies declared investments and exemptions under Section 80C and other sections, and spreads the tax liability across the remaining months of the financial year.

    **What StaffGlimpse generates:**

    - Monthly TDS computation with projected vs. actual comparison.
    - Quarterly TDS return (Form 24Q) data export.
    - Annual Form 16 (Part A and Part B) after the final payroll is closed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Labour Welfare Fund (LWF)">
    LWF is a state-specific contribution that supports welfare schemes for employees. StaffGlimpse applies LWF rules based on the state where the employee's work location is registered. Some states deduct LWF monthly; others deduct twice a year.

    **What StaffGlimpse generates:**

    - Monthly or periodic LWF deduction summary by state.
    - Consolidated statement for LWF board remittance.
    - Exemption tracking for employees above applicable wage limits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Compliance calendar

StaffGlimpse shows a compliance calendar in **Payroll > Compliance** with upcoming due dates for challan payments and return filings. The calendar is based on your organization's registered states and PF/ESI establishment codes.

<Tip>
  Configure your PT state and PF/ESI establishment details under **Organization Settings** before the first payroll run so deductions compute correctly from month one.
</Tip>

## Result

Statutory deductions are computed every payroll cycle, and the corresponding reports and challans are ready for filing. You can download them from the Compliance dashboard or set reminders for due dates.
