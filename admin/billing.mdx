StaffGlimpse billing is based on a per-employee-per-month model. Your subscription tier determines which modules are available (for example, Core HR, Payroll, or Performance). You can view your current plan, manage payment details, and change your subscription at any time from the Billing page.

<Note>
  Exact pricing depends on your selected plan and employee count. Contact your account manager or [sales@staffglimpse.com](mailto:sales@staffglimpse.com) for a custom quote if you need advanced modules, multi-entity payroll, or API access.
</Note>

## View your current plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Go to **Admin** > **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review plan details">
    The **Current Plan** card shows your tier, the active modules, your billing cycle (monthly or annual), and the next renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check employee count">
    The **Active Employees** count shows how many employees are currently billed. This number is updated automatically as you onboard or offboard employees.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payment settings">
    In **Admin** > **Billing**, click the **Payment Methods** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or edit a card">
    Click **Add Payment Method**. Enter card details or UPI information for India-based accounts. You can set a default method for automatic charges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove old methods">
    Click the trash icon next to any expired or unused payment method. You must keep at least one active method on file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open invoice history">
    Go to **Admin** > **Billing** > **Invoices**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and download">
    Use the date range filter to find past invoices. Click **Download PDF** next to any invoice to save it for your accounting records. Invoices include GST details for Indian customers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Compare plans">
    In **Admin** > **Billing**, click **Change Plan**. Review the feature comparison table to see which modules and limits are included in each tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new plan">
    Choose the plan that fits your needs. If you are upgrading, the prorated difference is charged immediately. If you are downgrading, the new rate applies at the next billing cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and activate">
    Review the summary and click **Confirm Change**. You receive a confirmation email, and new module access is enabled immediately for upgrades.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Your billing settings are updated, invoices are available for download, and any plan changes are reflected in your next billing cycle. HR and admin users retain full access based on the new plan limits.
