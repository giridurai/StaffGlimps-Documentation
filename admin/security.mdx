StaffGlimpse is built to keep your employee and payroll data safe. The platform offers enterprise-grade security controls that let you enforce strong authentication, limit access by network location, maintain a complete audit trail, and choose where your data is stored.

## Single sign-on (SSO) and SAML

You can connect StaffGlimpse to your corporate identity provider so employees log in with their existing work credentials.

- **SAML 2.0**: Connect providers such as Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, or any SAML-compatible IdP.
- **Just-in-time provisioning**: New users are auto-created on first login when SSO is enabled, reducing manual onboarding.
- **Force SSO**: Admins can disable password-based login entirely and require SSO for all users.

To configure SSO, go to **Admin** > **Security** > **SSO / SAML** and upload your IdP metadata XML file.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Add an extra layer of protection to every account.

- **TOTP apps**: Users can enroll any authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy) by scanning a QR code.
- **Enforcement policies**: Admins can require 2FA for all users, or only for Admin and Payroll Admin roles.
- **Recovery codes**: Each user receives backup codes during enrollment to regain access if they lose their device.

## IP allowlists

Restrict StaffGlimpse access to trusted office networks or VPN ranges.

- Add IPv4 or IPv6 ranges in **Admin** > **Security** > **IP Allowlist**.
- Users outside the allowed ranges are blocked at login and receive an email alert.
- You can maintain multiple allowlist entries for branch offices or remote VPN gateways.

## Data residency

Choose the geographic region where your tenant data is stored and processed.

- **India (Mumbai)**: Default for Indian customers. Supports statutory compliance requirements for local data storage.
- **Other regions**: Contact your account manager if you need data residency in a different region.

<Note>
  StaffGlimpse uses single-tenant data isolation at the database layer. Each organization gets a logically separated data store so employee records, payroll runs, and audit logs are never mixed between customers.
</Note>

## Audit logs

Track every significant action in your account for compliance and investigation.

- **Logged events**: Logins, password changes, role assignments, payroll runs, salary edits, employee exports, and setting changes.
- **Retention**: Audit logs are retained for the duration of your subscription. Enterprise plans can export logs to SIEM tools.
- **Access**: Go to **Admin** > **Security** > **Audit Logs** to search by user, action type, and date range.

## Data export and employee privacy

StaffGlimpse gives you control over data portability and employee rights.

- **Bulk export**: Admins can export employee data, payroll records, and attendance reports in CSV or Excel format from the respective module pages.
- **Employee data requests**: When an employee requests access to or deletion of their personal data, HR can generate a full data package from **Employee Management** > **Employees** > **Actions** > **Export Employee Data**.
- **Retention policies**: Offboarded employee data is retained for the statutory period required by local labor laws, then automatically purged according to your configured policy.

## Security checklist for admins

- [ ] Enforce 2FA for all Admin and Payroll Admin users.
- [ ] Configure IP allowlists if your team works from fixed office locations.
- [ ] Enable SSO to reduce password-related risks.
- [ ] Review audit logs monthly for unusual activity.
- [ ] Verify your data residency region matches your compliance requirements.
