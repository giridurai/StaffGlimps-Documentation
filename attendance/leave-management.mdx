StaffGlimpse leave management helps you define how time off is earned, requested, and approved across your organization. You can create policies for casual, sick, earned, comp-off, maternity, and other leave types, then set accrual schedules and carry-forward rules so balances stay accurate every pay period.

## Prerequisites

- You need an **Admin** or **HR Manager** role to create or edit leave policies.
- Employees must be active in the system to see leave balances and raise requests.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Admin: create policy">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open leave policies">
        Go to **Attendance > Leave > Policies** and click **New Policy**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose leave type">
        Select a base type such as Casual, Sick, Earned, Comp-off, or Maternity. You can also create a custom type if your organization needs something specific.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set eligibility">
        Define which employees or departments the policy applies to, and choose whether it is available from day one or after a probation period.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Configure accrual">
        Choose how leave is credited: monthly, quarterly, yearly, or a one-time grant. Enter the number of days per cycle and set a maximum balance cap if needed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set carry-forward and encashment">
        Decide how many unused days can carry into the next cycle, and whether excess days can be encashed at year end or during full and final settlement.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Define approval workflow">
        Select who must approve: direct manager, department head, HR, or a multi-level chain. You can also set auto-approval for requests below a certain number of days.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save and publish">
        Click **Save and Publish**. The policy becomes active for eligible employees, and their balances start updating based on the accrual schedule.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Employee: apply for leave">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open leave request">
        In the StaffGlimpse dashboard or mobile app, go to **Attendance > Leave > Apply**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select dates and type">
        Pick the start and end dates, then choose the leave type. The system shows your available balance for that type.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add reason and contact">
        Enter a reason for the leave. Some policies require an alternate contact or handover note. Fill those in if prompted.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Attach documents if needed">
        For medical or maternity leave, upload supporting documents. The file size limit is shown on the upload button.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Submit">
        Click **Submit Request**. Your manager and any additional approvers receive a notification.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Track status">
        Return to **Attendance > Leave > My Requests** to see pending, approved, or rejected applications. You can withdraw a request while it is still pending.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Manager approval workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open pending requests">
    Go to **Attendance > Leave > Approvals**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review details">
    Click a request to see the employee, leave type, dates, balance impact, and reason. Any attached documents are available in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    Click **Approve** to deduct the days from the balance, or **Reject** with a comment if the request does not meet policy rules. The employee is notified immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Employees see real-time leave balances, apply from web or mobile, and move through a clear approval chain. HR can audit policy usage, carry-forward balances, and encashment eligibility at any time.
