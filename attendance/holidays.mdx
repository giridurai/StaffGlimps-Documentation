StaffGlimpse lets you create holiday calendars that match the working culture and statutory requirements of each office location. By separating national, optional, and restricted holidays, you keep leave and payroll calculations accurate while giving employees clarity about their time off for the year.

## Prerequisites

- You need an **Admin** or **HR Manager** role to create or edit holiday calendars.
- Office locations must be configured in **Admin > Organization > Locations** before you can assign calendars.

## Create a holiday calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open calendars">
    Go to **Attendance > Holidays > Calendars** and click **New Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and assign locations">
    Enter a calendar name (for example, "Bangalore Office 2025") and select the locations that will use it. One location can only be linked to one active calendar per year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add holidays">
    Click **Add Holiday**. For each entry, provide:

    - **Date**: The exact calendar date.
    - **Name**: A short label such as Republic Day or Diwali.
    - **Type**: Choose **National**, **Optional**, or **Restricted**.
    - **Repeat yearly**: Toggle on if the holiday falls on the same date every year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the list">
    Verify the full list for the year. You can drag rows to reorder, or use the bulk import option if you have a CSV prepared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the calendar">
    Click **Save**. The calendar is stored as a draft until you publish it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Publish the calendar to employees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the draft calendar">
    In **Attendance > Holidays > Calendars**, select the calendar you created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview employee view">
    Click **Preview** to see how the calendar will appear in the employee dashboard and mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. Employees at the linked locations receive an in-app notification, and the calendar becomes visible in their **Attendance > Holidays** view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notify stakeholders">
    Use the **Notify Managers** button to send a summary email to department heads so they can plan team coverage around key dates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage multiple location calendars

If your organization has offices in different states or cities, create a separate calendar for each location. This is important because optional and restricted holidays often vary by region, and payroll statutory rules such as Professional Tax can depend on local working days.

<Note>
  When you assign a new calendar to a location that already has an active calendar for the same year, the previous calendar is archived. Historical holiday data is preserved for attendance and payroll audits.
</Note>

## Result

Employees at each location see a clear, accurate holiday list in their dashboard and mobile app. HR and payroll teams can rely on the calendar to compute leave balances, loss of pay, and statutory filings without manual adjustments.
